🌌 How to Read the Patterns

Speed = How fast the pattern moves
• Slow drift = Low solar wind speed (calm)
• Rapid flow = High solar wind speed (active!)

Colors = Magnetic field conditions
Purple/Magenta = Aurora conditions! (BZ southward)
Orange/Gold = Quiet conditions (BZ northward)

Wind & Ocean patterns react directly to solar wind speed - watch how fast things move!
